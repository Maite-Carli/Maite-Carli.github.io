## Outreach

-["Atelier Alkindi"](https://www.epfl.ch/education/education-and-science-outreach/fr/classes-primaires-secondaires-1/cryptographie-en-classe/) (Winter semester 2021-2022)  
Together with other Bachelor and Master students from EPFL, I conducted workshops on
cryptography in Swiss secondary schools all over Switzerland, designed and organised by
EPFL's [science outreach department](https://www.epfl.ch/education/education-and-science-outreach/science-outreach/).
Bringing the wonders of maths to middle schoolers was a lot of fun and it is an
experience I would love to renew!
